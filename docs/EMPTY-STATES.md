# If they did not log it, do not show it

A standing rule for the whole app, set 2026-10-29.

## The rule

A section with nothing to report **renders nothing**. Not a heading, not an explanation of the
absence, not an invitation to go and tag more. The section is simply not there.

## Why

Two reasons, and the second is the one that keeps getting forgotten.

**Not everyone wants to log everything.** Tagging break, reads, strike, faults and cup entries is
optional and always will be. Somebody who logs distances and results is using the app correctly,
and every panel explaining what they are missing tells them they are using it wrong.

**Empty states make an app look bare.** Ten headings over ten paragraphs about data that is not
there reads as unfinished software, however carefully each paragraph is written. The version with
four real sections and no apologies looks better and *is* better.

## Never ask for data the player did not offer

Stating a limit is honest. Issuing an instruction is pressure, and the two read very differently
on a page somebody opened to look at their putting.

    state it      Only 23 misses have a side on them, which is thin for a pattern.
    do not        Tag a few more rounds before reading into it.

    state it      Only a putt struck badly and holed anyway can separate the two.
    do not        Tag a few putts you struck badly and holed anyway and this can start answering.

Same fact, and the first does not tell anyone they are holding the app wrong. If a number needs
something the record does not contain, say what the number cannot do. Do not assign homework.

## Nothing is ever deleted

Hiding is a display decision and only a display decision. Every field stays in the model, every
tagged value stays on the putt, and the moment something is logged it appears everywhere it
belongs with no migration and no prompt. A player who tags nothing for a year and then tags one
round gets that round's sections, immediately, in every place they are supposed to be.

## What this is not

It is not hiding a number behind a tap, and it is not softening a caveat. Where a figure is on
screen it still says what it stands on, still refuses when the data cannot support it, and still
explains the refusal in the place it refuses. Phase 2 is unchanged.

The distinction is simple: **nothing logged means nothing drawn. Something logged means the truth
about it, including the awkward parts.**

## Where it applies today

- Cup entry, room to spare, and how much of the hole you use: no entries, no section.
- The execution model: nothing judged, no section. Once something is judged it appears and states
  its own limits, including a refusal when the fault variable is standing in for the outcome.
- Drift: below the pair threshold the heading goes with the panel.
- Bailed, Robbed and Sold: no verdicts, no section.
- xPOT on a round or a putt: only where it has a number.
- Make chance beyond the longest putt ever hit: no number.

## What was removed to get here

A "finish the tagging" list on the round page, which told the player which putts were incomplete.
It also mislabelled fully tagged putts as unfinished, because the flag it keyed on only existed on
putts logged after it shipped. Both problems had the same root: the app was grading the player's
record-keeping rather than their putting.
