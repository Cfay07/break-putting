# What BREAK! is

Set by Conor on 2026-11-07, after a long back and forth with ChatGPT about DECADE, Arccos and
18 Birdies. The build order in [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md) does not change. This file is the filter
every feature on that order has to pass.

## The line

**Treat every player like a pro.**

Three promises sit under it. A feature earns its place by serving at least one.

1. **The best golf stats app.** Every number says what it stands on, and the app never claims
   something the data cannot support. Depth comes from the putting engine first, then full game.
2. **The best app for getting better.** Find the costliest leak, give a drill for that exact
   putt or shot, measure it with the weekly test, and check it on course.
3. **The pro treatment for every golfer.** A 25 handicap gets the same round card, player page
   and presentation as a college player. The analysis adapts to the player. The quality does not.

Do all three well and it is the best golf app, full stop. That is the ambition, not a claim to
make in public before it is shown.

## What it is not

- An Instagram for golfers. The feed (build step 4) is moments from real round data, each one
  linking back to the round.
- A GPS rangefinder or a course-map business.
- A leaderboard app. Live team scores may come with step 4, but they are a feature, not the
  identity. Undecided.
- A pile of stats nobody reads. Removal stays allowed. Collapsing stays banned.
- Generic AI golf advice. Every suggestion comes from the player's own numbers.

## Where the ChatGPT write-ups conflict with standing rules

These were in the pasted plans and are ruled out unless Conor reverses them:

- **Offseason mode.** He rejected separate modes on 2026-10-30 ("what if someone's playing in
  Florida"). Practice already adapts to how recent the rounds are, on one screen.
- **Near-black and purple redesign.** BREAK!'s look stays its own: paper, deep green, one orange
  accent. "Pro treatment" means presentation quality, not a new palette.
- **Projected scores, invented player-form numbers in mockups.** Nothing ships showing a number
  the app did not measure.
- **Strategy and course management (DECADE's core).** Not on the build order. Revisit after full
  game has a season of data.
- **Physical training, mental training, swing video.** Out of scope.
