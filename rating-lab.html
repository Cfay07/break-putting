Fixed · per-putt credit was double counting

A hole where the second putt also breaks. My card summed each putt's own adjustment, which charges the leftover putt's difficulty twice.

The fix is the telescoping form, credit_i = c_i − c_(i+1), with zero after hole-out.

My own example hid it. The second putt was straight and flat, so the double count added zero. One breaking leftover and it is off by a quarter of a stroke.

What the fix bought

Putt 1's credit comes out negative even though it was a breaking 22 footer. It gets charged for the breaking downhill six footer it left behind. That is new.
